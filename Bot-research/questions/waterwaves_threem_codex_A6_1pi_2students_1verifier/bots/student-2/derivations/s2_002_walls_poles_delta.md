# Subset walls, factorization channels, and the common denominator

## Candidate subset walls

Write

\[
q_S=gk_S=\sum_{p\in S\cap P}\omega_p^2-
\sum_{m\in S\cap M}\omega_m^2.
\]

Using \(q_{S^c}=-q_S\), every nontrivial possible zero is in one of two
\(S_3\times S_3\) orbits:

\[
\begin{aligned}
q_{mp}&=\omega_p^2-\omega_m^2,
&&m\in M,\ p\in P \quad (9\text{ labeled walls}),\\
q_{m;pq}&=\omega_p^2+\omega_q^2-\omega_m^2,
&&m\in M,\ \{p,q\}\subset P \quad (9\text{ labeled walls}).
\end{aligned}
\]

All size-four and size-five cases are complements of these, and same-sign
pairs or triples cannot have zero momentum.  The pair walls change the
eight-word label.  Triple-wall signs can vary while the word is fixed, so they
must be represented by separate orbit bricks rather than by enlarging the
base word table.

For example, both exact points below have \(W=+--++-\), while the displayed
triple sign changes:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
(a;b,c,d,e;f)&q_{b;de}=d^2+e^2-b^2\\ \hline
(24/5;1,4,2,3;26/5)&12\\
(24/5;4,1,2,3;26/5)&-3.
\end{array}
\]

An exact path crossing only this representative triple wall locally is
\[
(b,c,d,e)=(t,2,3,4),\qquad t=5,
\]
with \(q_{b;de}=25-t^2\).  Near \(t=5\) its word remains \(+--++-\).

## Propagator factorization

For \(T=\{m,p,q\}\) containing one minus and two plus legs, the dimensionless
inverse propagator numerator is

\[
d_T=\omega_T^2-|q_T|,\qquad
q_T=\omega_p^2+\omega_q^2-\omega_m^2.
\]

Direct algebra gives

\[
d_T=
\begin{cases}
2(\omega_m+\omega_p)(\omega_m+\omega_q),&q_T>0,\\[2mm]
2\!\left[
\omega_p^2+\omega_q^2+\omega_p\omega_q+
\omega_m(\omega_p+\omega_q)\right],&q_T<0.
\end{cases}
\]

For \(q_T<0\), cutting the line makes a four-point one-minus amplitude on one
side (and its global-sign-flipped one-plus partner on the other).  The known
one-minus vanishing therefore forces the would-be residue to cancel in the
complete six-point amplitude.  For \(q_T>0\), both sides are two-minus
four-point sectors, so only this sign can contribute a factorization/residue
brick.  This explains structurally why the rejected baseline organized its
extra pieces as \(\sum_{q_T>0}J_T\).

The union of possible surviving linear factors is consequently generated by
\(\omega_m+\omega_p\).  The natural fixed common denominator is

\[
\boxed{\Delta=\prod_{m\in M}\prod_{p\in P}(\omega_m+\omega_p).}
\]

It has degree \(9\), so \(N_W=-iA_6\,\Delta\) has homogeneous degree \(17\).
This degree matches the size of the rejected chamber numerator tables and
provides a sharply testable normalization for future interpolation.

## Are the candidate poles genuine on shell?

If \(\omega_m+\omega_p=0\), then that mixed pair cancels in both energy and
momentum.  The remaining four frequencies satisfy

\[
x+y+u+v=0,\qquad x^2+y^2=u^2+v^2,
\]

with two legs of each momentum sign.  Hence
\[
u+v=-(x+y),\qquad uv=xy,
\]
so \(\{u,v\}=\{-x,-y\}\): all three mixed pairs cancel.  Thus a zero of one
factor of \(\Delta\) is not an isolated nondegenerate factorization locus; it
lies on the fully pair-cancelled degenerate boundary.

Exact BG approaches to
\[
(-2,-3,-5,2,3,5)
\]
were made with free frequencies
\[
(\omega_2,\omega_3,\omega_4,\omega_5)
=(-3,-5,2+t,3+\alpha t)
\]
and solved \(\omega_1,\omega_6\).  For
\(\alpha=-1,1,3,5\) and \(t=\pm1/320\), \(|A_6/i|\) stayed between
\(1.25\times10^6\) and \(1.32\times10^6\); for \(\alpha=2\), the two-sided
sequence at \(|t|=1/40,1/80,1/160,1/320\) converged toward approximately
\(1.286\times10^6\), with no \(1/t\) growth.  This is strong evidence that the
apparent \(\Delta\)-poles are removable on the on-shell manifold.

Status: the algebraic location of the pole locus and the denominator-clearing
checks are exact.  Removal is numerically supported but still needs an exact
cleared-numerator divisibility proof (or the final compact formula).
