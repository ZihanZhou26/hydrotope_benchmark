# The order-three \(Q\)-wall brick

## Convention and target

For \(m\in M=\{1,2,3\}\) and \(\{p,q\}\subset P=\{4,5,6\}\), set
\[
Q_{m;pq}=\omega_p^2+\omega_q^2-\omega_m^2.
\]
The orientation used throughout is
\[
\Delta_{m;pq}R
 =R_{\rm spline}\big|_{Q_{m;pq}>0}
  -R_{\rm spline}\big|_{Q_{m;pq}<0}
 =Q_{m;pq}^{\,3}G_{m;pq}.
\]

## Representative extraction

The worked isolated line
\[
\omega(t)=(8,2,-3,-5,4,-6)
+t(-2,1,0,2,-1,0),\qquad u=t-\frac14,
\]
has
\[
Q_{1;46}=12u.
\]
It is exactly on shell because all five line constraints
\[
\sum_iP_i=\sum_id_i=\sum_i\sigma_iP_i^2
=\sum_i\sigma_iP_id_i=\sum_i\sigma_id_i^2=0
\]
hold.  In the two-sided sampling window the ascending labeled magnitude order is
\((2,3,5,4,6,1)\), all nine \(q_{mp}\) signs are fixed, and the only changing
triple sign is \(Q_{1;46}\).
The exact two-branch jump polynomial previously reconstructed independently by
the verifier is
\[
\Delta_{1;46}R
=-1555200u^3+829440u^4-110592u^5.
\]
It therefore gives
\[
\frac{\Delta_{1;46}R}{Q_{1;46}^3}
=-900+480u-64u^2
=-16\left(\frac{15}{2}-2u\right)^2
=-16\omega_1(t)^2.
\]
This fixes the brick on the worked cell to
\[
\left.G_{m;pq}\right|_{\omega_m^2>\omega_t^2}
=-16\,\omega_m^2,
\]
where \(t\) is the omitted plus leg.

## Global factored brick and selector

Fresh exact crossings in the other pair-ordering show the complementary
quadratic \(-16\omega_t^2\).  All cells are generated by the single selector
\[
\boxed{
G_{m;pq}
=-16\max(\omega_m^2,\omega_t^2)
=-16\left[\omega_m^2+
  \bigl(\omega_t^2-\omega_m^2\bigr)_+\right],
\qquad
\{t\}=P\setminus\{p,q\}.}
\]
Thus \(G\) is not one global polynomial: it is a degree-two spline across the
single finer pair wall \(q_{mt}=\omega_t^2-\omega_m^2=0\).  There is no energy
sign or other \(Q\)-wall selector.  Stabilizer symmetry under
\(p\leftrightarrow q\) and exchange of the two non-primary minus legs is
manifest.

Equivalently, the entire order-three orbit integrates independently as
\[
R_Q=-16\sum_{m=1}^{3}\ \sum_{\substack{p<q\\p,q\in\{4,5,6\}}}
\bigl(Q_{m;pq}\bigr)_+^3
\left[\omega_m^2+
\bigl(\omega_t^2-\omega_m^2\bigr)_+\right],
\qquad \{t\}=P\setminus\{p,q\}.
\]
Crossing one isolated \(Q_{m;pq}=0\) changes only its corresponding summand and
reproduces the oriented branch difference above.
In the amplitude normalization of the question, this orbit contributes
\(i\,g^{-3}R_Q\) to \(A_6\).

This has the required degree \(2+3\cdot2=8\).  It is one nested positive-part
brick with nine symmetry images, not a chamber lookup.  With the convention
\((0)_+^3=0\), it is continuous together with its first two normal derivatives;
the third normal derivative has the finite nonzero jump.  Thus \(Q=0\) is a
regular subset-momentum chamber wall and this term introduces no denominator or
genuine pole.
At an intersection of several \(Q\)-walls the prescription is termwise and
unambiguous.  The value on a wall is defined by this continuous limit; the BG
oracle itself must not be evaluated there.

The nested term
\[
-16\,(Q_{m;pq})_+^3(q_{mt})_+
\]
also makes the coupling to the order-one pair fan explicit.  At \(q_{mt}=0\)
the brick itself is continuous but its first derivative changes.  Consequently
one cannot assemble the \(q\)- and \(Q\)-wall orbits as independent polynomial
bricks; their compact inclusion-exclusion is already present in this product of
truncated powers.

## Assembly consequence

Writing the orbit as
\[
R_Q=
-16\sum_{m,p<q}\omega_m^2(Q_{m;pq})_+^3
-16\sum_{m,p<q}(Q_{m;pq})_+^3(q_{mt})_+
\]
isolates the coupling.  Across a fixed pair wall \(q_{mt}=0\), only the term
whose plus pair is \(P\setminus\{t\}\) contributes the additional oriented
order-one coefficient
\[
-16\,(Q_{m;P\setminus\{t\}})_+^3.
\]
Therefore the natural next assembly step is to subtract \(R_Q\) from
\(R_{\rm spline}\) and transport the remainder over the magnitude fan, with
this explicit degree-six correction removed from the old \(H_{mt}\).  If the
eighteen-wall set is complete, the subtraction removes every \(Q\)-wall jump.

The order-three coefficient is also intrinsic to the amplitude, rather than an
artifact of the settled pole subtraction.  For generic external frequencies,
the active \(P_{\rm pole}\) channel has two factors
\(\mathcal H(Q;\cdot,\cdot)=Q\) as \(Q\to0^+\), in addition to its explicit
\(Q^2\).  Its one-sided onset is therefore \(O(Q^4)\) and cannot change the
\(Q^3G\) coefficient.

## Limits

On the wall,
\[
G_{m;pq}
=-16\max(\omega_p^2+\omega_q^2,\omega_t^2).
\]
It scales as \(-16\) times the larger of the primary-minus and omitted-plus
squared frequencies.  In either hierarchy the dominant one supplies the
quadratic.  At their same-energy wall \(q_{mt}=0\), the common two-sided value is
\(-16\omega_m^2=-16\omega_t^2\), so the brick has no value discontinuity.
