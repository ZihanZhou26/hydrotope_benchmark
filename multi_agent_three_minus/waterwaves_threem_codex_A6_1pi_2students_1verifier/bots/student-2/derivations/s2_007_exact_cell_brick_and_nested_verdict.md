# Exact degree-six wall brick and the nested-spline verdict

## 1. Exact quotient-ring reconstruction

Set \(w_i=\omega_i\), eliminate
\[
w_6=-(w_1+w_2+w_3+w_4+w_5),
\]
and impose the remaining homogeneous quadratic conservation relation
\[
-w_1^2-w_2^2-w_3^2+w_4^2+w_5^2+w_6^2=0.
\]
Its leading term can be chosen to be \(w_5^2\).  A homogeneous normal
basis at degree \(d\) is therefore
\[
\mathcal B_d=\{w_1^{a_1}\cdots w_5^{a_5}:
\textstyle\sum_i a_i=d,\ a_5\leq1\}.
\]
In particular,
\[
|\mathcal B_8|=\binom{12}{4}-\binom{10}{4}=285,\qquad
|\mathcal B_6|=\binom{10}{4}-\binom{8}{4}=140.
\]

I reconstructed the two analytic polynomial branches of
\[
R=A_6/i-P_{\rm pole}
\]
on both sides of \(q_{24}=w_4^2-w_2^2=0\).  For integral free
coordinates \((b,c,d,e)\), I used the integral scaled coordinates
\(W_i=S w_i\), \(S=b+c+d+e\), so homogeneity gives
\(R(W)=S^8R(w)\).  Each branch was determined from a full-rank
\(285\times285\) exact system and then checked at 20 fresh exact BG
points.

Three wall environments were reconstructed:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
(B,c,e),\ b=t,\ d=B-t & \operatorname{rank}(R_L,R_R)&
\text{fresh holdouts}&\text{normalized }H_{24}\\ \hline
(10,2,3)&(285,285)&40/40&h\\
(5,2,3)&(285,285)&40/40&h\\
(-30,4,-5)&(285,285)&40/40&h .
\end{array}
\]
Here the intrinsic orientation is always
\[
R(q_{24}>0)-R(q_{24}<0)=q_{24}H_{24}.
\]
All divisions had exact zero remainder.  At the first wall point
\[
w=(-23/3,5,2,5,3,-22/3)
\]
the recovered polynomial gives
\[
H_{24}(w)=\frac{12622720}{27},
\]
exactly reproducing the independent round-2 one-variable limit.

## 2. Explicit representative ground truth

After relabeling \(1\leftrightarrow2\), the representative \(H_{14}\)
in the first analytic environment is \(32h_{14}\), where
\[
\begin{aligned}
h_{14}={}&
w_1^4w_2w_4+w_1^4w_3^2-w_1^3w_2w_3^2-w_1^3w_3^3
-w_1^2w_2^2w_3^2-4w_1^2w_2w_3^3-w_1^2w_2w_3^2w_4\\
&-2w_1^2w_2w_4^3-w_1^2w_3^4-2w_1^2w_3^3w_4
-3w_1^2w_3^2w_4^2-w_1w_2^3w_3^2-2w_1w_2^2w_3^3\\
&-w_1w_2^2w_3^2w_4-2w_1w_2w_3^4-3w_1w_2w_3^3w_4
-2w_1w_2w_3^2w_4^2-w_1w_3^5-w_1w_3^4w_4\\
&-2w_1w_3^3w_4^2-2w_1w_3^2w_4^3-w_2^3w_3^3
+w_2^2w_3^3w_4-w_2w_3^5+w_2w_3^4w_4\\
&+w_2w_3^3w_4^2-2w_2w_3^2w_4^3+w_2w_4^5-w_3^3w_4^3 .
\end{aligned}
\]
This is exact ground truth, not proposed as the desired compact final
formula.  It has 29 monomials and content \(32\).  Exact factor tests
find no factor \(w_i\pm w_j\), no factor \(C\), and SymPy finds no
nonconstant factor over \(\mathbb Q\).  It is invariant under the
residual plus swap \(5\leftrightarrow6\) after on-shell reduction, but
is not invariant under the residual minus swap \(2\leftrightarrow3\).

The intrinsic restriction to the primary wall is much shorter.  Put
\[
a=w_1,\qquad x=w_2,\qquad y=w_3,\qquad s=x+y,\qquad v=xy,
\]
where in this environment \(y\) is the smaller-magnitude remaining
minus leg.  Exact reduction of the displayed polynomial gives
\[
\boxed{
\left.H_{14}\right|_{w_4=a}
=-32y^2\left[
4a^4+6a^3s+2a^2(s^2+v)+(as+v)(s^2-2v)
\right]}
\]
on the same-energy component, and
\[
\boxed{
\left.H_{14}\right|_{w_4=-a}
=-32y^2\left[as^3+v(s^2-2v)\right]}
\]
on the opposite-energy component.  Both symbolic residuals are exactly
zero.  These five-block/two-block wall traces are substantially more
compact than an off-wall polynomial extension and are the natural
ground truth for matching a nested-hydrotope construction.

There is also a short exact **off-wall** form for this entire analytic
piece.  Define
\[
q=p^2-a^2,\qquad p=w_4,
\]
\[
F_-=as^3+v(s^2-2v),\qquad
D=2a^3+3a^2s+a(s^2+v)-sv,
\]
\[
L=3a^2+2a(s+p)-v+p(2x+y).
\]
Then the 29-term polynomial above is identically
\[
\boxed{
H_{14}
=-32y^2\!\left[F_-+(a+p)D\right]
-32q\,y^2L+32xp\,q^2 .}
\]
The exact multivariate residual is zero.  Thus even before the global
selector is known, one analytic \(H_{14}\) piece is generated by only
the three short blocks \(F_-,D,L\) and powers of \(q\).

The second reconstructed intrinsic piece occurs when the minimizing
non-primary leg is an **other plus** leg \(z\).  It is symmetric in the
two remaining minus legs through \(s=x+y\), \(v=xy\), and has the exact
form
\[
\boxed{H_{14}^{(+\beta)}
=-32z^2[F_-+(a+p)D]+32qK_+,}
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
K_+={}&A_0+sA_1+s^2A_2+vB_0+svB_1,\\
A_0={}&a^4+4a^3p+4a^3z+4a^2p^2+6a^2pz+ap^3+2ap^2z,\\
A_1={}&4a^3+8a^2p+7a^2z+5ap^2+7apz+p^3+p^2z,\\
A_2={}&3a^2+4ap+3az+p^2+pz,\\
B_0={}&3a^2+2ap+az,\qquad B_1=3a+p .
\end{aligned}
\]
After on-shell reduction, this seven-block expression equals the
independently reconstructed 109-term analytic polynomial exactly.
Together, the minus-\(\beta\) and plus-\(\beta\) formulas reproduce all
four fully reconstructed \(H_{14}\) contexts (three of the first type,
one of the second) with zero multivariate residual.

The distinguished square in the same-energy wall trace has a compact
global-looking selector.  For a general same-energy wall \(w_p=w_m\),
let
\[
\beta=\min_{j\notin\{m,p\}}|w_j|.
\]
For the representative \(q_{24}=0\), set
\[
a=w_2=w_4,\qquad s=w_1+w_3,\qquad v=w_1w_3.
\]
The exact candidate
\[
\boxed{
H_{24}\big|_{w_4=w_2}
=-32\beta^2\left[
4a^4+6a^3s+2a^2(s^2+v)+(as+v)(s^2-2v)
\right]}
\]
passed \(17/17\) distinct isolated wall environments and \(102/102\)
fresh branch-polynomial holdouts.  The minimizing leg covered all four
non-primary possibilities (legs \(1,3,5,6\)), and the environments
covered five sorted words.  The earlier rule using only the smaller
remaining minus leg failed in \(6\) of these \(17\) environments; the
four-leg minimum fixes every failure.

This formula exactly explains student-1's independent affine exchange.
On their slice
\[
w_2=w_4=5,\qquad w_3=u,\qquad w_5=6-u,
\]
the solved remaining frequencies are
\[
w_1=\frac{3u-73}{8},\qquad
w_6=-\frac{3u+55}{8}.
\]
When \(\beta=|u|\), the boxed trace reduces identically to
\[
P(u)=-\frac{u^2}{16}
\left(219u^4-2628u^3+55226u^2-284052u-2037485\right),
\]
precisely the independently interpolated polynomial in claim
\(s1\_004\).  When the minimizing leg exchanges to
\(w_5=6-u\), the same compact trace reduces identically to
\(P(6-u)\).  Both symbolic residuals are zero.  Thus the observed
branch exchange is exactly the four-leg \(\beta\)-minimum selector,
not merely a coincidental fit at the shared anchor.

The opposite-energy locus \(w_p=-w_m\) is not an isolated pair wall:
direct two-sided paths necessarily changed several other pair/triple
signs at once.  The two exact cell-polynomial restrictions support the
two-block expression with the corresponding cell's \(\beta\), but it
was not assigned an independent physical jump by the scan.  It should
therefore be treated as a limiting value of the final nested formula,
not as a separately verified one-wall prescription.

## 3. The brick is itself a spline

The last asymmetry is intrinsic, not an interpolation artifact.  In the
original \(H_{24}\) labeling, swap the two non-primary minus legs
\(1\leftrightarrow3\).  This keeps the primary wall \(q_{24}=0\) fixed
and maps the first physical wall environment to another physical wall
environment.  Dual \(S_3\) covariance therefore fixes its wall
coefficient to be
\[
H_{24}^{\rm swapped}(w)=H_{24}(w_3,w_2,w_1,w_4,w_5).
\]
Exact comparison gives
\[
H_{24}^{\rm swapped}-H_{24}\ne0;
\]
the coefficient of \(w_1^5w_2\) in the difference is \(-32\), and the
full difference has 50 monomials.  Thus no single polynomial
\(H_{24}\), and hence no single residual-symmetric polynomial
\(H_{14}\), works throughout the physical domain:
\[
\boxed{H_{mp}\text{ is itself a spline.}}
\]
The base and symmetry-related environments differ in the eight finer
signs
\[
q_{14},q_{15},q_{16},Q_{1;45},
\qquad
q_{34},q_{35},q_{36},Q_{3;45}.
\]
This is a coupled higher-codimension change.  Two isolated adjacent
tests are useful negative controls:

* crossing only \(q_{25}\) leaves the normalized \(H_{24}\) exactly
  unchanged;
* crossing only \(Q_{1;45}\) leaves it exactly unchanged.

Consequently the missing structure is not a simple secondary term
\(q_{25,+}K\) or \(Q_{1;45,+}K\) in this component: both exact
sub-bricks are zero.  A successful compact formula must organize
coupled/nested wall data (or use an equivalent sorted/orbit
construction), not the single-orbit ansatz
\(\sum q_{mp,+}H_{mp}^{\rm polynomial}\).

## 4. Exact obstruction to naive integration

The compact intrinsic pieces do **not** yet assemble by the naive rule
\[
R_{\rm trial}=R_0+\sum_{m,p}q_{mp,+}H_{mp}^{(\beta)}.
\]
I constructed \(R_0^{\rm cell}=R_{\rm cell}-\sum q_{mp,+}H_{mp}^{(\beta)}\)
from all eight fully reconstructed branch polynomials.  Although the
two compact \(H\) types match their four source contexts exactly, only
\(1/8\) candidate \(R_0^{\rm cell}\) equals the chosen reference.
Every inequality is an exact nonzero polynomial identity, not a
numerical residual.

The mechanism is visible already at the \(q_{24}\) wall: its flip can
also exchange the minimizing leg inside another intrinsic coefficient
such as \(H_{35}\).  Adding the separately extracted intrinsic jumps
therefore double-counts a coupled change.  The remaining mathematical
step is a Möbius/inclusion--exclusion integration over the coupled
minimum fan (or an equivalent direct min/determinant construction), not
the discovery of the local wall blocks themselves.
