## 1. The formula

Label the two "minus" legs ($\sigma_i=-1$) as legs $1,2$ with frequencies
$\omega_1,\omega_2$. Then the tree amplitude in the two-minus sector is

$$
\boxed{\;A_n \;=\; i\,\cdot\,2^{\,n-1}\,\,g^{\,3-n}\,\,(\omega_1\omega_2)\,
\big[\min(\omega_1^2,\,\omega_2^2)\big]^{\,n-3}\;}
\qquad (n\ge 4).
$$

Equivalently, writing $\omega_<,\omega_>$ for the minus-leg frequencies of
smaller / larger absolute value,

$$
A_n \;=\; i\,\cdot 2^{\,n-1}\,g^{\,3-n}\;\omega_>\;\omega_<^{\,2n-5}.
$$

Striking features:

* **$A_n$ depends only on the two minus legs.** The $n-2$ plus-leg frequencies
  enter *only* through the on-shell constraints (which fix $\omega_1,\omega_2$
  once the plus legs are given); they do not appear explicitly.
* **Dimensions / scaling:** homogeneous of degree $2n-4$ in the frequencies and
  $\propto g^{3-n}$, both verified directly (§3).
* The amplitude is **purely imaginary** (an overall $i$), as for every value
  produced by `BGAmplitude` in this sector.

### Worked smallest case
$n=5$, free $\{\omega_2,\omega_3,\omega_4\}=\{2,3,5\}$ gives
$\omega=\{-\tfrac{13}{2},2,3,5,-\tfrac72\}$, so $\omega_1\omega_2=-13$,
$\min(\omega_1^2,\omega_2^2)=\min(\tfrac{169}{4},4)=4$, and
$$A_5 = i\,2^{4}\,(-13)\,(4)^{2} = -3328\,i,$$
matching `BGAmplitude` exactly.

## 3. How it was derived

1. **Scaling & dimensions.** Because $|k_i|=\omega_i^2/g$ regardless of $\sigma$,
   tracking powers through the BG vertex/propagator gives $A_n$ homogeneous of
   degree $2n-4$ in $\omega$ and $\propto g^{3-n}$. Confirmed numerically:
   scaling $\omega\to\lambda\omega$ multiplied $A_5$ by $\lambda^{6}$, and
   $A_5(g)\propto g^{-2}$.

2. **Symmetry.** `BGAmplitude` is invariant under permuting the two minus legs
   and (separately) the $n-2$ plus legs — checked directly. So $A_n$ is a
   function of the symmetric data of each group.

3. **Exact data + fit.** I generated 64 exact rational amplitudes for
   $n=5,6,7$ (`gen_dataset.m` → `data.txt`). A symmetric-polynomial fit failed,
   showing $A_n$ is **not** polynomial — it is rational with a genuine
   denominator.

4. **Symbolic extraction.** Because the only non-analyticity is `mag[k]=|k|`,
   I computed $A_n$ symbolically by resolving every `Abs` via its sign at a
   reference point (`sym5.m`, `symN.m`). In the chamber sampled by ordered free
   frequencies this collapses to
   $A_n/i = 2^{n-1}\,\omega_1\,\omega_2^{\,2n-5}$ — depending only on the minus
   legs. The 64-point data then matches $2^{n-1}(\omega_1\omega_2)
   \min(\omega_1^2,\omega_2^2)^{n-3}$ identically (the $\min$ restores the
   $1\leftrightarrow2$ symmetry: whichever minus leg has the smaller $|\omega|$
   carries the high power $2n-5$, confirmed with dedicated sign/magnitude tests).

5. **$n=4$ base case** handled by a limit (§4).

## 4. The $n=4$ base case

At $n=4$ the two on-shell constraints force $\{\omega_1,\omega_2\}=\{-\omega_3,
-\omega_4\}$ as a set, which puts an internal line *exactly* on-shell — `BGAmplitude`
returns `Indeterminate` ($0/0$ in a propagator). The singularity is **removable**:
deforming slightly off momentum conservation by $\epsilon$ and letting
$\epsilon\to0$ gives a finite limit. For $(\omega_3,\omega_4)=(3,2),(5,2),(7,3)$
the limits are $192i,320i,1512i$, exactly the formula
$A_4=i\,2^{3}g^{-1}\omega_1\omega_2\min(\omega_1^2,\omega_2^2)$.

## 5. Scope (honest statement)

The boxed formula is **exact whenever a minus leg carries the smallest
$|$momentum$|$**, i.e. $\min(\omega_1^2,\omega_2^2)\le\omega_j^2$ for every plus
leg $j$. This is the generic/physical regime: it holds for **all** kinematics
produced by `MakeKinematics` with ordered free frequencies (every test in
`OnShellBG.m`), and it survives the non-generic regimes of a very large/small
*minus* frequency or a very large *plus* frequency.

Because the BG kernels contain $|k|$ (the dispersion $\omega^2=g|k|$ is
non-analytic in $k$), the *full* amplitude is **piecewise**-rational: in the
non-physical chambers where a *plus* leg is the softest, $A_n$ takes a different
rational form (e.g. for $n=5$, two soft plus legs $a,b$ give
$A_5/i = 2^{5}\,\omega_1\omega_2\,|k_a||k_b|$ with $|k|=\omega^2$). These
chamber forms were mapped (`scan5.m`, `inter5.m`); a single globally-analytic
expression does not exist, which is why the boxed result is the natural
"closed-form amplitude" — the direct analogue of a Parke–Taylor / MHV formula
for this theory. The companion notebook flags the regime via
`in_physical_regime`.
